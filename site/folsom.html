Folsom Collection

A mix of Chinese porcelain and American stoneware, brought together around the theme of bondage. During a ceramics residency in Jingdezhen, I found myself drawn to taking such a fragile material as porcelain and turning it into light, playful objects in warm glazes. The theme became the avenue for me to explore mold making, slip casting, throwing and gas reduction firing while in Jingdezhen. This constrained the work and allowed me to create a thematic collection by the end of the residency. These pieces either sold at the Folsom Street Fair in 2026 or are available online.
