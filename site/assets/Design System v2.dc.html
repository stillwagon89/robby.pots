Your four images are the keyframes, and they set the art direction the rest should match: cobalt linework on off-white paper, drafting-drawing weight. That reference drives the whole system — it is where the cobalt ramp and the hairline-not-fill rule come from. To animate, each transition needs roughly 6 tween frames between two keyframes: 4 × 6 = 24 more. Prompt them on a plain white ground with no desk or surrounding sheets, so they need no cutting out. Sequence them with animation-timing-function: steps(7) so frames snap rather than blend.

WHERE THE BLUES COME FROM
Cobalt oxide fires blue in a single narrow band, which is why qinghua ware from Jingdezhen has one hue and many dilutions rather than a spread of colors. The ramp above follows that logic: a single hue at 228°, varied only by lightness. Nothing else is introduced except the four desaturated status colors, which exist for form feedback and never appear in editorial layout.
