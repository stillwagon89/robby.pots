## Summary of the lookback (about 25 searches + direct site reads, 2026-10-04)

- **Wood/soda/salt dated opportunities verified Oct 2025 – Oct 2026:** 5 (Sonoma soda Jan; Oribe/Uncle Paul's soda Jul–Oct; Laney 12-week wood; Mendocino Safe Stokes Oct; Cobb SodaScape Oct). Cobb says it runs 6–10 wood workshops a year, but its page shows only upcoming ones, so past ones in the window are invisible.
- **Wood/soda in the next 90 days (Oct 4 – Jan 2):** 4 (Mendocino Oct 19, Cobb Oct 7, Cobb Nov 25, Cobb Jan 8). **All 4 are sold out or waitlisted.**
- **Raku/pit dated opportunities found Oct 2025 – Oct 2026:** about 10 (6 of them from one place, Manhattan Beach Art Center).
- **Salt:** zero California salt-firing workshops found.
- **Places verified to let outside potters into wood/soda firing:** about 9–10 (Cobb Mountain, Mendocino Art Center, Laney, Sonoma Community Center, Oribe Firing Center, Flynn Creek, Mendocino College, CSU Sacramento, Calimpong, Idyllwild from 2027). Almost all in Northern California; Southern California wood access barely appears online.
- **Supply is the constraint:** all 5 of Cobb's firing workshops are waitlisted (6 of its 8 listings overall); Mendocino's October wood workshop is sold out.
- **Findability:** Cobb points people to its newsletter and Instagram for firing info. Mendocino's workshop pages 404 after the event. General web search surfaces raku mostly from one LA-area city program. Bay Area raku searches came back nearly empty, which almost certainly reflects indexing, not supply.
