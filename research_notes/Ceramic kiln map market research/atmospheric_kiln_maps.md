# Existing Maps, Directories and Registries of Wood-Fire, Soda and Salt (Atmospheric) Kiln Locations

Research date: 2026-10-04. Method: web search, direct site visits, and direct download of the KML exports behind the Google My Maps (counts below come from parsing those KML files, not from claims on the sites). Reddit and Instagram could not be accessed (Reddit blocks both the search tool and its JSON API; hashtag-count sites hide numbers behind a paywall), so social metrics are mostly gaps.

## What wood-fire kiln maps/directories exist, and how big, fresh and popular are they?

### Takeaway
The niche has only a handful of resources, all small and run by one person: Simon Levin's World Wood Kiln Map (a Google My Map with 209 pins, mostly US, with no dated entry after 2019), Flash & Ash (a crowd-sourced Wix directory of roughly 100+ wood kilns, with a beta map of 84), and SideStoke (an Australian site whose creator died in 2020, with no updates since about 2010). The one large, actively maintained kiln directory is Kiln Share (1,288 kilns), but it is a general kiln-rental marketplace and is not organized around atmospheric firing.

### Cited Findings

**1. World Wood Kiln Map (Simon Levin), simonlevin.com/map**
- Run by Simon Levin, a wood-fire potter. It describes itself as "A public resource of wood fired kilns around the world" and accepts "any type of wood kiln for pottery" — [Simon Levin map page](https://www.simonlevin.com/map)
- Kiln types are color-coded: Anagama (blue), Fast fire (yellow), Train (green), Cross draft (red), Groundhog (purple), Noborigama (white). There is no soda or salt category — [Simon Levin map page](https://www.simonlevin.com/map)
- Listings are curated. There is no form: owners email Simon@SimonLevin.com with kiln name, type, size in cubic feet, construction date, contact details and website — [Simon Levin map page](https://www.simonlevin.com/map)
- The embedded map is Google My Maps ID `1I77OQo5qCeurI5h1XP_Kf8Y6azs`, titled "Wood Kilns". Its KML export holds **209 placemarks**. By coordinates: about 163 in the contiguous US, 17 in Europe, 12 in Australia, 3 in New Zealand, 3 in South Africa, 4 in Alaska, 2 in Canada, and single pins in Thailand, Timor-Leste, Singapore, Taiwan and Japan/Korea. Japan has essentially no coverage — [KML export](https://www.google.com/maps/d/kml?mid=1I77OQo5qCeurI5h1XP_Kf8Y6azs&forcekml=1) (my own parse)
- Freshness: the latest year mentioned in any placemark description is **2019** (years mentioned cluster from 2008 to 2015). The page shows no "last updated" date — [KML export](https://www.google.com/maps/d/kml?mid=1I77OQo5qCeurI5h1XP_Kf8Y6azs&forcekml=1); [Simon Levin map page](https://www.simonlevin.com/map)
- Popularity: American Craft Council coverage describes Levin as having been "crowdsourcing a searchable map of wood kilns." The article returned 403 on direct fetch, so its date and any counts in it are unverified — [craftcouncil.org search snippet](https://craftcouncil.org/articles/kilns-that-build-community/)
- Flash & Ash points users to "Simon Levin's kiln map" for interactive mapping — [Flash & Ash](https://www.flashandash.com/)

**2. SideStoke World Map of Woodfire Kilns, sidestoke.com/worldmap.html**
- The map is managed by Simon Levin (USA) with Josh Harmony (USA) and Arthur Rosser (Australia). It is "an active Google map with markers for wood-fired kilns." Submissions go by email to one of the three coordinators and must include coordinates — [SideStoke world map page](https://www.sidestoke.com/worldmap.html)
- It appears to be the same Levin map: SideStoke has no separate map ID, and Levin manages both.
- SideStoke also hosts a separate "Map of Australian woodfirers" and a "Coming events" page — [SideStoke home](https://www.sidestoke.com/)
- **Stale.** Creator Arthur Rosser died in August 2020, and his son Matthew now monitors the email. The newest dated content on the homepage is March 2010, with copyright 2005. The National Library of Australia selected the site for preservation — [SideStoke home](https://www.sidestoke.com/)
- Arthur and Carol Rosser were North Queensland wood-fire and salt potters who built an anagama and the "Oztrain" kiln — [Ceramic Arts Queensland, Influential Woodfirers](https://ceramicartsqld.org.au/influential-woodfirers/)

**3. Flash & Ash, flashandash.com**
- A "crowd-sourced record of wood kilns" created and run by Any Guelmann, a ceramic artist in Detroit, as a passion project. It aims to survey and map the woodfiring community worldwide. Listings come "through research, word of mouth, and submissions," and adding one is free (flashandash.com/add) — [Flash & Ash](https://www.flashandash.com/)
- Format: a text directory at /kilns. Each card shows kiln name, kiln type, owner/operator and region/country, and links to a detail page. The directory runs to about 8 pages, roughly 100+ kilns. There is a "NEW! filter by tags" feature. No dates, firing schedules or crew signup are shown — [Flash & Ash kilns](https://www.flashandash.com/kilns)
- Type tags in the /kilns page include anagama, noborigama, train, fast fire, single chamber, groundhog, catenary, cross draft, bourry, **soda** and **salt**. This makes it the only wood-focused directory found that tags soda and salt. (These are counts of term mentions in the page HTML, not listing counts) — [Flash & Ash kilns](https://www.flashandash.com/kilns) (my own parse)
- The beta map page says "**84 kilns mapped**" — [Flash & Ash map](https://www.flashandash.com/map)
- The homepage also embeds an older copy of the "Wood Kilns" Google My Map (ID `17eYnGjozsA5nm9LCjZmAGtGC1fQ`) with **152 placemarks** (about 117 in the US, 12 in Europe, 12 in Australia). The latest year mentioned in it is 2013, so it looks like an old snapshot of Levin's map — [KML export](https://www.google.com/maps/d/kml?mid=17eYnGjozsA5nm9LCjZmAGtGC1fQ&forcekml=1) (my own parse)
- Freshness: copyright 2025. The site's Wix revision number is 1911, which suggests frequent edits. There are Instagram @flash.and.ash and #flashandash, an email list and a Printful merch store. Follower counts were not obtainable — [Flash & Ash](https://www.flashandash.com/)

**4. Kiln Share, kilnshare.com (general kiln rental, not atmospheric-specific)**
- "A global kiln directory connecting potters looking to rent kiln space with local kiln owners." It shows **1,288 active kilns**, "40+ countries" and about **15,000 monthly visitors** (self-reported). It is free for both sides with no commission, has an interactive flame-icon map, in-platform messaging, 11 languages and a blog — [Kiln Share](https://www.kilnshare.com/en/); [Kiln Share search](https://www.kilnshare.com/en/search/)
- It launched in April 2021, founded by "Mike," a hobby potter without a kiln, with 40+ kilns in 4 countries at launch — [Scottish Potters Association](https://www.scottishpotters.org/news/new-kiln-sharing-platform-launches/)
- The search page has no kiln-type filter (wood/soda/salt/raku/gas/electric). Neither the WebFetch summary nor a grep of the page HTML found any type terms — [Kiln Share search](https://www.kilnshare.com/en/search/)

**5. Regional events and tours that act as partial "maps"**
- Woodfire NC is a conference run by Starworks (a nonprofit in Star, NC). The 2025 edition ("Sustain") ran May 22–25, 2025, and the site says it "returns in 2028." The site has no kiln map or directory — [Woodfire NC](https://www.woodfirenc.com)
- The Seagrove Wood Fire Studio Tour (NC) is scheduled for June 6–7, 2026, with a printed or PDF tour map of about a dozen participating studios (Ben Owen, Jugtown, Luck's Ware, Studio Touya and others). It covers one town and is tied to the event — [Discover Seagrove](https://discoverseagrove.com/events/seagrove-wood-fire-nc-main-event); [Seagrove area map PDF](https://discoverseagrove.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/NEW-SAPA2020-map.pdf)
- Australia: the Fire & Clay Festival at Montsalvat (Victoria) ran 15–21 June 2026 as "a seven-day intensive celebration of wood-fired" ceramics — [ArtsHub](https://www.artshub.com.au/event/montsalvat-festival-of-fire-clay-2855527/). Woodfire Tasmania was a one-off international event convened by Neil Hoffmann in Deloraine in 2011, with no evidence of a standing directory — [Tas Craft Fair](https://tascraftfair.com.au/exhibitors/neil-hoffmann)

**6. Japan kiln villages (local tourism lists, not kiln-type maps)**
- Mashiko: more than 440 potters and kiln studios. The Mashiko-yaki Kyohan Center sells work from about 250 kilns — [tabi-mag / mapple search results](https://www.mapple.net/collection/4a1f64ee6f904d52ad06cc0cc390ed07/). An English-language source puts Mashiko at "more than 380" potters — [KEK Mashiko PDF](https://www2.kek.jp/rso/test/Activities/Travel/pdf/Mashiko.pdf)
- Bizen: about 300 operating kilns in the early 21st century — [Wikipedia, Bizen ware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bizen_ware)
- Shigaraki: the industry cooperative (shigaraki.ne.jp) and the tourism association publish kiln-walk routes (four "窯元散策路" courses past working noborigama) — [Shiga Prefecture](https://www.pref.shiga.lg.jp/fd00/6496.html); [Shigaraki Model Course PDF](https://www.e-shigaraki.org/discovershigaraki/.assets/Shigaraki-Model-Coures.pdf)

### Inferences
- The best-known global wood-kiln map (Levin) is effectively stale. No placemark mentions a year after 2019, it relies on email submission to one person, and it uses the free Google My Maps layer with no search, filters or schedules. Its 209 pins are a small fraction of the real number of wood kilns (Bizen alone had about 300 kilns), and it is heavily US-weighted (about 78% contiguous US).
- Flash & Ash is the most actively maintained wood-specific directory, but it is small (about 100+ listings, 84 mapped) and is a hobby project on Wix.
- The only large, active, well-trafficked directory (Kiln Share, 1,288 kilns, about 15k visits/month) has no kiln-type filtering, so it does not serve the "find a wood/soda/salt kiln" need.
- No resource surfaced offers firing schedules, crew signup or "open firing slot" features. In practice those live on individual kiln websites and in social media.

### Gaps
- Similarweb traffic for simonlevin.com, flashandash.com and kilnshare.com was not obtained (no access). Kiln Share's 15k/month figure is self-reported.
- Flash & Ash's exact listing count (the directory paginates) and its Instagram follower count were not verified.
- The "Map of Australian woodfirers" on SideStoke was not opened, so its pin count is unknown.
- No "Wood Fire Collective" organization or "International Woodfire Conference" with a directory was found. Searches returned only forest-fire conferences.
- UK-specific wood-kiln lists were not found in this pass.

## What soda/salt kiln directories exist?

### Takeaway
I found no dedicated soda or salt kiln directory or map anywhere. The closest are Flash & Ash, which tags some kilns "soda" and "salt" within its wood directory, and Kiln Share, which lists kilns of every type but cannot filter by type.

### Cited Findings
- Searches for soda/salt kiln directories returned only technique articles (Digitalfire, Ceramic Arts Network) and forum threads, not location lists — [Digitalfire glossary](https://digitalfire.com/glossary/salt+and+soda+firing); [Ceramic Arts Network](https://ceramicartsnetwork.org/category/firing-techniques/salt-firing-and-soda-firing/)
- Ceramic Arts Daily forum advice to newcomers is to find "someone with a soda or salt kiln." In other words, discovery happens person to person — [CAD forum thread 18935](https://community.ceramicartsdaily.org/topic/18935-lots-of-questions-about-soda-firing/)
- Levin's map has no soda/salt category — [Simon Levin map page](https://www.simonlevin.com/map)
- Flash & Ash includes "soda" and "salt" tags — [Flash & Ash kilns](https://www.flashandash.com/kilns)
- UK: Errington Reay (Bardon Mill) is described as the only commercial pottery in the UK licensed for salt glaze. There is no UK salt-kiln list — [Wikipedia, Salt glaze pottery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_glaze_pottery)

### Inferences
- Soda and salt kilns are unmapped as a category. This is a clear white space, and soda kilns are far more common in US university and community studios than wood kilns.

### Gaps
- I did not check whether university ceramics programs or NCECA publish facility lists that include soda/salt kilns.

## Are there kiln listings inside other platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Reddit, forums)?

### Takeaway
Discovery clearly happens on social platforms, but I could not verify member or post counts in this session. Reddit is blocked to the research tools, and hashtag-count services paywall their numbers.

### Cited Findings
- Flash & Ash runs Instagram @flash.and.ash and the hashtag #flashandash — [Flash & Ash](https://www.flashandash.com/)
- Flick.social hides #woodfired and #anagama post counts behind "Unlock Metrics" — [Flick #woodfired](https://www.flick.social/learn/hashtags/woodfired); [Flick #anagama](https://www.flick.social/learn/hashtags/anagama)
- Reddit returned an access error to both the search tool and the JSON API, so no threads or subscriber counts could be retrieved (tool limitation, not evidence of absence).
- Community wood kilns often organize their own firings and crews (for example, Spring Valley Anagama, a 100 cu ft community anagama started in 2000) — [woodkiln.org](http://www.woodkiln.org/)

### Inferences
- Firing-crew recruitment and slot-filling appear to happen in closed or semi-closed channels: Facebook groups, Instagram DMs and kiln-owner email lists. None of these can be searched by location.

### Gaps
- Facebook group names and member counts (for example, "Wood Fired Pottery" and "Soda Firing" groups) were not verified, because Facebook is login-walled.
- Instagram post counts for #woodfired, #anagama, #woodfiredpottery, #sodafired and #saltglazed were not obtained. Check them manually in the Instagram app.
- Reddit r/Pottery and r/Ceramics subscriber counts and "wood fire near me" threads were not retrieved.

## Is there any universal map covering all kiln types together?

### Takeaway
No universal atmospheric-kiln map exists. Kiln Share is the only global map spanning every kiln type, but it is a rental marketplace with no type filtering. The wood-specific maps leave out soda and salt (Levin) or are small (Flash & Ash).

### Cited Findings
- Kiln Share: 1,288 kilns, 40+ countries, map plus messaging, free. It has no kiln-type filter, and listings are framed around renting space, not firing type, events or crews — [Kiln Share](https://www.kilnshare.com/en/); [Kiln Share search](https://www.kilnshare.com/en/search/)
- Levin map: wood only, six wood-kiln categories, 209 pins, no entries dated after 2019 — [Simon Levin map](https://www.simonlevin.com/map); [KML](https://www.google.com/maps/d/kml?mid=1I77OQo5qCeurI5h1XP_Kf8Y6azs&forcekml=1)
- Flash & Ash: wood plus soda/salt tags, about 100+ listed, 84 mapped, text-first — [Flash & Ash](https://www.flashandash.com/); [Flash & Ash map](https://www.flashandash.com/map)

### Inferences
- What is missing in the market: one searchable, type-filterable map (anagama / noborigama / train / groundhog / soda / salt / raku / pit), self-serve owner listings, freshness signals (last confirmed date), firing calendars and crew or slot signup.
- The Levin and Flash & Ash datasets combined probably total only a few hundred unique wood kilns, mostly in the US.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether any Japanese, Korean or European national directory goes beyond local tourism lists.
