# General-Purpose Platforms for Studio, Kiln Access, Firing Services, Workshops and Residencies

Research date: 2026-10-04. Scope: the broader directory/marketplace landscape, not limited to wood/soda/salt. About 20 search/fetch calls. Several platforms named in the brief (Mudshare, ClayShare, Pottery Studio Map, Potters Council, Peerspace, ClassPass, individual school sites like Penland/Haystack/Arrowmont) could not be checked within the call budget, or turned up no results. They are listed under Gaps rather than guessed at.

## 1. Studio, community-studio and kiln-access finders

### Takeaway
The only dedicated, global kiln-access directory with real traction is **Kiln Share** (kilnshare.com): free, no commission, about 1,288 kilns in 40+ countries, about 15k monthly visitors. Its listing form collects "Kiln type", but there is no evidence that kiln type is a map filter. Other studio finders, such as FindAPotteryClass.com (US) and GetPotteryClass.com (UK), are class- and studio-oriented and organize listings by technique or service ("raku", "Outside Firing Services"). None of them classify kilns by atmosphere (wood/soda/salt). Google Maps and Yelp remain the default "pottery near me" tools, with no kiln metadata.

### Cited Findings
**Kiln Share (kilnshare.com)**
- Free global kiln directory that connects potters with local kiln owners. Launched April 2021 by a "kiln-less" hobby potter. — [Scottish Potters Association](https://www.scottishpotters.org/news/new-kiln-sharing-platform-launches/)
- Created by Mike McManus. As of January 2022 it had 250 kilns in 30 countries, "over 6000 potters" visiting that month, and "hundreds of firing requests". Listings require a kiln photo, zip/postcode and firing rules. — [Ceramic Arts Network / Ceramics Monthly archive](https://ceramicartsnetwork.org/daily/article/no-kiln--no-problem!-online-directory-connects-potters-with-local-kiln-owners)
- The homepage (fetched Oct 2026) claims 1,288 live kilns, 40+ countries and 15,000 monthly visitors, with a footer dated © 2025. It says it does not charge commission or process rental fees. Users search through an interactive map, and the page names no filter options. — [kilnshare.com](https://kilnshare.com)
- Milestone: "1000 listed kilns on the global map across more than 40 countries" and "over 15,000 monthly visitors". — [Kiln Share via search snippet / kilnshare.com/en/about](https://www.kilnshare.com/en/about/)
- The "List Your Kiln" form asks for "Kiln brand & model", "Kiln type", "Internal size", "Firing temperatures", rules and a photo. It is "100% FREE" and owners set their own prices and payment terms. No paid tiers are mentioned. — [Kiln Share sign-up](https://www.kilnshare.com/en/sign-up/)
- Built by a UK freelance web developer (portfolio entry "Kiln Share » Mike the Freelancer"). — [mikethefreelancer.co.uk](https://mikethefreelancer.co.uk/work/kiln-share/) (seen in search results only, not fetched)
- Covered by Ceramics Monthly ("Clay Culture") and Pottery Making Illustrated ("Side Hustles: Kiln Share"). L&L Kilns (hotkilns.com) links to it from its support resources. — [CM](https://ceramicartsnetwork.org/ceramics-monthly/ceramics-monthly-article/clay-culture-kiln-share); [PMI](https://ceramicartsnetwork.org/pottery-making-illustrated/pottery-making-illustrated-article/in-the-studio-side-hustles-kiln-share); [L&L Kilns](https://hotkilns.com/support/pottery-kiln-videos-links/kilnshare)
- Has a Facebook page, @kilnshare. Follower count not captured. — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/kilnshare/)

**KilnSpace (Bullseye Glass)**
- Glass-focused analog: an interactive map with search filters that connects Bullseye glass users who have kilns to rent with renters. Bullseye Resource Centers list their own kilns, and guests otherwise contact owners directly. — [KilnSpace About](https://kilnspace.bullseyeglass.com/about/); [KilnSpace FAQ](https://kilnspace.bullseyeglass.com/faq/)
- This shows the "kiln-sharing map" model sponsored by a manufacturer as a side channel.

**FindAPotteryClass.com (US)**
- Interactive map search by zip/city with tag filters. The tags cover class types, techniques (wheel, handbuilding, **raku**, paint-your-own), adult/kids, birthday/private events, **"Outside Firing Services"**, open studio and memberships. It has a "List My Studio" CTA, and listings appear to be free. No wood/soda/salt/gas/electric filters. Operator and listing count were not disclosed on the page. — [findapotteryclass.com/find-studios](https://findapotteryclass.com/find-studios/)

**GetPotteryClass.com (UK)**
- UK directory with "over 800 pottery studios and classes". Business model includes **featured placement** for studios (paid visibility). — [parse.gl brand profile](https://parse.gl/brands/getpotteryclass-com) (secondary source)

**Ceramics NZ directory (New Zealand)**
- A national association directory exists at directory.ceramicsnz.org. Fields were not examined. — [directory.ceramicsnz.org](https://directory.ceramicsnz.org/)

**Class marketplaces (CourseHorse etc.)**
- CourseHorse lists pottery studios and classes (e.g., Pottery Studio 1 and Pottery NYC in New York; LA kids' ceramics). Classes cost about $90–$130, with filters for neighborhood, price and class type. It runs a commission/booking marketplace for classes, not kiln access. — [CourseHorse Pottery Studio 1](https://coursehorse.com/nyc/schools/art/pottery-studio-1); [Brownstoner](https://www.brownstoner.com/brooklyn-life/coursehorse-shows-you-classes-to-take-in-brooklyn)

**Google Maps / Yelp**
- Consumer guides consistently point to Google Maps and Yelp as the primary way to find studios, by rating, price and reviews. — [The Pottery Wheel](https://thepotterywheel.com/10-easy-ways-to-find-a-pottery-firing-service-near-me/)
- The same guide suggests community centers, technical colleges and art schools as sources of kiln firing services, which tells you these are not centrally listed anywhere. — [The Pottery Wheel](https://thepotterywheel.com/10-easy-ways-to-find-a-pottery-firing-service-near-me/)

**London kiln scarcity (UK context)**
- Cluster London: studios that can fire ceramics are "few and far between" in London, with only a handful of kilns and a couple of firing services (e.g., The Kiln Rooms in Peckham, London Sculpture Workshop). — [Cluster London](https://www.cluster-london.com/get-fired-up-article-cluster-crafts-2020)

**Adjacent: studio-management SaaS (they hold kiln data but are not public finders)**
- Kiln Fire (launched 2023): class registration, membership billing, firing fees, POS. Kilnfox (since early 2025): piece tracking, equipment bookings, memberships. Others include Ceramik, PotteryStudio.app and the 24hr Pottery member app ($3.99/user/month). — [ClayTrack comparison 2026](https://claytrack.com/blog/best-pottery-studio-software/); [Ceramics Now on Kiln Fire](https://www.ceramicsnow.org/news/introducing-kiln-fire-a-new-app-for-pottery-studio-management/); [Ceramik](https://ceramik.app/); [24hr Pottery](https://www.24hrpottery.com/)

**Dead result**
- theceramicshop.com/content/23841/studio-directory/ now resolves to an e-commerce catalog, not a studio directory. Any studio directory that once lived there is gone or moved. — [The Ceramic Shop](https://www.theceramicshop.com/content/23841/studio-directory/)

### Inferences
- Kiln Share already collects kiln type as free-text or a field, so it could add a type filter. A unified kiln map should treat it as the main integration or competition target. It has strong SEO and press from Ceramic Arts Network, but its value is in electric-kiln hire for hobbyists, not atmospheric firing.
- Every studio finder found is free or freemium (featured placement). Commission models appear only in class marketplaces (CourseHorse) and are absent from kiln access. That suggests kiln access is too low-value or too informal for transaction fees.
- None of the finders separates "wood/soda/salt" or "atmospheric" as a category. The closest proxy is a "raku" tag, which leaves a clear niche gap.
- Studio-management SaaS (Kiln Fire, Kilnfox, Ceramik) holds structured data about each studio's kilns and firing fees. A future integration route could be pulling public "firing services" data from those systems.

### Gaps
- Kiln Share: could not confirm whether "kiln type" is a searchable map filter or only appears on listing pages. Its breakdown by kiln type (how many gas/wood kilns) is unknown.
- No Similarweb or traffic data was retrieved for any platform beyond Kiln Share's self-reported 15k/month.
- Not checked: Pottery Studio Map, Mudshare, ClayShare, Potters Council member directory, NCECA resources, Peerspace or Airbnb-style studio rental, ClassPass pottery coverage, the Clayscapes directory, the Australian Ceramics Association, and Canadian provincial guild directories.
- No operator or listing count was found for FindAPotteryClass.com.

## 2. Workshop and residency directories: do they filter by kiln/firing type?

### Takeaway
The major residency databases (Alliance of Artists Communities, TransArtists, Res Artis) and Ceramics Monthly's annual residency list all treat ceramics as a discipline. At most they offer generic kiln facets. The Alliance directory's equipment facets name "Raku Kiln, Electric Kiln, Gas Kiln", with **no wood, soda or salt facet**. Ceramics Monthly mentions wood or anagama kilns only in free text inside individual entries.

### Cited Findings
- **Ceramics Monthly 2026 Residencies and Fellowships**: 100+ programs. Fields are org/location, contact, program, duration, deadline, application fee, eligibility, residency fees, stipend, and opportunities/responsibilities. Firing types show up only in prose, e.g., Cobb Mountain "Wood-Fire Residency", wood-kiln management at Red Lodge and Sawtooth, and Peters Valley "firing kilns including an anagama". They are "not systematically filterable". CM also runs a separate "Summer Workshops" listing with a submission form. — [CM 2026 residencies](https://ceramicartsnetwork.org/ceramics-monthly/ceramics-monthly-article/2026-residencies-and-fellowships); [CM residencies resource](https://ceramicartsnetwork.org/ceramics-monthly/ceramics-monthly-resources/residencies)
- Organizations self-submit to CM's annual listing through an online form, so it is a free, editorial, once-a-year publication. — [CM 2015 listing note](https://ceramicartsnetwork.org/ceramics-monthly/ceramics-monthly-article/Residencies-and-Fellowships-2015)
- **Alliance of Artists Communities directory** (artistcommunities.org): 20+ pages of residencies. Facets cover location, program type, context (urban/rural), 60+ disciplines including "Ceramics/Clay Arts/Pottery", funding/accessibility, and facilities. Its equipment facet lists "Raku Kiln, Electric Kiln, Gas Kiln", with no wood/soda/salt filter. — [AAC directory](https://artistcommunities.org/directory/residencies)
- Archie Bray Foundation is an Alliance member. — [Wikipedia: Alliance of Artists Communities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_of_Artists_Communities)
- **Res Artis**: founded 1993, with 700+ vetted member residencies in 85+ countries. — [Wikipedia: Res Artis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Res_Artis)
- **TransArtists** (Amsterdam): searchable by artist, discipline ("Textiles & Ceramics & Crafts") and place. Kiln details appear only in free text on listings, e.g., ARS Ceramics Residency lists a 150 L gas kiln and 20/60/250 L electric kilns. — [TransArtists Clay Studio](https://www.transartists.org/en/air/clay-studio); [TransArtists ARS Ceramics](https://transartists.org/en/air/ars-ceramics-residency); [Mediamatic on TransArtists](https://mediamatic.net/ar/id/36255)
- Ceramics Now (Substack) publishes a curated "list of residencies for ceramic artists", another editorial list with no faceting. — [Ceramics Now #48](https://ceramicsnow.substack.com/p/48-a-list-of-residencies-for-ceramic)

### Inferences
- Residency and workshop discovery is split between annual editorial lists (CM, Ceramics Now) and multidisciplinary databases (AAC, TransArtists, Res Artis). None models firing type as data. Someone looking for "a wood-fire residency" or "a salt-firing workshop" has to read prose entries one by one. A kiln map that links residencies and workshops to specific kiln records (type, atmosphere, size) would fill a real gap.
- AAC's existing "Gas/Electric/Raku" facet shows that directory owners will accept kiln facets, so proposing extra values (wood, soda, salt) to them is a plausible integration path.

### Gaps
- Did not inspect individual school sites (Penland, Haystack, Arrowmont, Anderson Ranch, Archie Bray, Watershed, Medalta, Alfred) to see whether their workshop catalogs filter by firing type. No source was found showing that any of them do.
- No exact listing totals for the AAC directory or the TransArtists database.
- Not verified whether Res Artis's member search has facility or kiln facets.
- The structure of CM's Summer Workshops listing was not retrieved.

## 3. Kiln-sharing / "Airbnb for kilns" startups: past and present

### Takeaway
No venture-backed "Airbnb for kilns" was found on Kickstarter, Product Hunt or in search results. The space is held by **Kiln Share**, a free hobby-founder directory that has grown from 250 kilns (Jan 2022) to about 1,288 (2025–26) with no commission model, and by the glass-world counterpart **KilnSpace** (Bullseye). That pattern suggests transactional kiln marketplaces have not proven viable or have not been tried at scale.

### Cited Findings
- Searches for Kickstarter or Product Hunt campaigns for kiln sharing found none, and Kiln Share has grown as a free directory. — [Kiln Share](https://www.kilnshare.com/en/); [Kiln Share About](https://www.kilnshare.com/en/about/)
- Kiln Share explicitly avoids payments: "DOES NOT take commission or decide how much you charge – we simply connect potters with kiln owners." — [Kiln Share sign-up](https://www.kilnshare.com/en/sign-up/)
- Growth trajectory: 250 kilns in 30 countries (Jan 2022) to 1,000+ in 40+ countries, then 1,288 in 40+ countries (current homepage). Monthly visitors went from about 6,000 to about 15,000. — [CAN archive](https://ceramicartsnetwork.org/daily/article/no-kiln--no-problem!-online-directory-connects-potters-with-local-kiln-owners); [kilnshare.com](https://kilnshare.com)
- KilnSpace (Bullseye Glass): kiln-rental map for glass, run by a manufacturer. Its FAQ separates "Kiln Guest" and owner roles, with direct contact rather than in-platform payment. — [KilnSpace FAQ](https://kilnspace.bullseyeglass.com/faq/); [Kiln Guest FAQ](https://kilnspace.bullseyeglass.com/?p=1476)
- Context on studio fragility: Clay By The Bay (Fisherman's Wharf, SF) shut down abruptly in July 2026. Community-studio listings go stale quickly. — [Hoodline, Jul 2026](https://hoodline.com/2026/07/clay-by-the-bay-kicked-to-the-curb-as-fisherman-s-wharf-pottery-hub-shuts-overnight-6730227/)

### Inferences
- The free, contact-only model has worked for growth, while the commission model has no visible examples. A unified kiln map would likely need to be free to list, with revenue (if any) coming from sponsorship (e.g., kiln or clay suppliers, as Bullseye does with KilnSpace) or featured listings (as GetPotteryClass does).
- Studio closures like Clay By The Bay make freshness a key differentiator. None of the platforms reviewed shows visible "last verified" dates.

### Gaps
- Crunchbase and app stores were not checked directly. Earlier failed attempts (e.g., anything named "ClayShare" or "Mudshare") were neither confirmed nor ruled out.
- No app-download or social-follower numbers were collected for any platform.
